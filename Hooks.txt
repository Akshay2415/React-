useState - state ko manage krne ke liye

useEffect - side effect handle krne ke liye
            (jaise API call , DOM manupulation, event listner)

useContext - global state ko consume 
            karne ke liye without prop drilling

useReducer - complex state management ke liye (redux jaisa chota version)

useRef - mutable values hold krne ke liye jo re-render trigger na karien ,
         ya DOM access krne ke liye

useMemo & useCallback - optimization ke liye, 
            unnecessary re renders avoid krne ke liye.
